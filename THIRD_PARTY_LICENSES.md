No third-party images, audio samples, fonts or shader code are bundled. All GLSL shaders, geometry
algorithms and music are original to this repository. Techniques are credited where they were learned:
cosine palettes (Inigo Quilez, https://iquilezles.org/articles/palettes/), the `sdStar` signed-distance
formulation (Inigo Quilez, MIT-licensed examples), and value-noise/fBm as taught in The Book of Shaders
(https://thebookofshaders.com/).

Cultural sources (Shipibo-Konibo kené, Islamic geometric art, Anatolian and Persian carpets, Hindu and
Tibetan mandalas, the Sri Yantra) inspire original procedural interpretations; no traditional artwork is
reproduced.
