# Image credits & licences

Images are bundled in `public/art/` as WebP (≤1600 px, quality 72; 360 px thumbnails in `public/art/thumb/`; square crops in `public/art/tex/` are used as WebGL textures and atmospheric backdrops).
Every licence was checked through the Wikimedia Commons API (`extmetadata.LicenseShortName`/`LicenseUrl`) when downloaded (November 2026). Only public-domain, CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA files are used.
**Adaptations:** the crops, colour-mapped textures and kaleidoscopic/tunnel renderings derived from the CC BY-SA images (marked below) are shared under the same licence (CC BY-SA, same version) with attribution to the original authors. CC BY images are credited to their authors as required.
Shipibo-Konibo textile photographs are shown as reference images and used for palette inspiration only. They are not distorted into shader textures, out of respect for living Indigenous designs. Copyrighted art (for example Pablo Amaringo's paintings or 1960s posters) is **not** included.

# Soundtrack

All ten soundtrack clips in `public/audio/` are **original compositions synthesised for this project** (Python/NumPy
additive, modal and formant synthesis; scripts in `tools/audio/`). No samples,
field recordings or existing melodies are used; “icaro-like”, “maqam-flavoured”, “raga-flavoured” and “Shur-flavoured”
describe the general style only. Released with the site under the MIT licence.
