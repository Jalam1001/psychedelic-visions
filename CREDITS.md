# Image credits & licences

All images below are bundled in `public/art/` (downscaled to ≤1024 px; 512 px square crops in `public/art/tex/` are used as shader textures).
Every licence was verified via the Wikimedia Commons API (`extmetadata.LicenseShortName`) at download time (November 2026).
Only public-domain / CC0 works are embedded. Shipibo-Konibo textile photographs are shown as reference images and used for palette inspiration only — they are not distorted into shader textures, out of respect for living Indigenous designs.
Copyrighted art (e.g. Pablo Amaringo’s paintings, 1960s psychedelic posters) is **not** included; at most it informed general colour mood.

# Soundtrack

All ten soundtrack clips in `public/audio/` are **original compositions synthesised for this project** (Python/NumPy
additive, modal and formant synthesis; scripts in `tools/audio/`). No samples,
field recordings or existing melodies are used; “icaro-like”, “maqam-flavoured”, “raga-flavoured” and “Shur-flavoured”
describe the general style only. Released with the site under the MIT licence.
